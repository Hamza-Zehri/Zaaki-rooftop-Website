## Brand & Style

This design system encapsulates the warmth, heritage, and celebratory hospitality of Afghan and Pakistani culinary traditions, elevated by the open-sky ambiance of a high-altitude rooftop in Khuzdar. 

The aesthetic is **Traditional-Modern Luxe**: combining the gravitas of historical regional banquets with razor-sharp modern dining ergonomics. The UI evokes the sensation of dusk turning into a starlit Khuzdar night—glowing charcoal hearths, burnished brass samovars, cast-iron platters, and intimate amber illumination.

### Key Tenets
- **Atmospheric Nocturne:** Deep obsidian and charcoal backdrops dominate, reducing eye fatigue while making vibrant food photography and golden typography glow organically.
- **Crafted Metallics:** Gold and amber are deployed not as gaudy textures, but as refined framing lines, luminous badges, and glowing highlights that evoke polished copper tableware and evening lanterns.
- **Sensory Resonance:** Warm terracotta, crimson saffron, and ivory bring the warmth of rich charcoal kebabs, aged basmati, and ceremonial hospitality directly into digital touchpoints.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for an opulent dark-mode-first environment, reflecting an evening dining experience under open desert skies.

- **Primary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm Saffron Amber. Utilized for primary interactive states, key callouts, selected navigation indicators, and luminous decorative highlights.
- **Secondary (`#B45309`):** Deep Spiced Ochre. Grounds interactive states, secondary buttons, warm border accents, and badge backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#991B1B`):** Tandoor Crimson / Spice Ruby. Reserved for VIP badges, promotional banners, hot signature items, and critical alerts.
- **Neutral (`#121316`):** Hearth Charcoal Base. Sets the deep, non-glare night ambiance for the root viewport, paired with `#1A1C22` (elevated surface) and `#232730` (card surface).

### Foreground & Typography Color Tokens
- **Headings & High-Emphasis Text:** Ivory Warmth (`#FDFBF7`)
- **Body & Supporting Copy:** Warm Slate Muted (`#9CA3AF`)
- **Subtle Metadata & Icons:** Raw Stone (`#6B7280`)
- **Accents & Hairlines:** Burnished Gold Border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`)

## Typography

The typographical pairing balances dramatic editorial prestige with modern computational clarity:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes timeless hospitality, royal culinary heritage, and literary menus. High-contrast serifs bring distinction to dish names, culinary narratives, and section banners.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean legibility across ingredient lists, pricing, reservation matrices, and operational data. Its contemporary geometric curves harmonize with the serif's high-fashion grace.
- **Casing & Microcopy:** Labels, category markers, and rooftop status tags default to uppercase with tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury hospitality signboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to frame food imagery and reservation modules like framed artworks.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 56px (`3.5rem`) outer margins. Max layout width constrained to `1320px` for optimal viewing distance.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px baseline grid (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`). Generous negative space between editorial menu collections creates an unhurried, fine-dining rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering combined with soft, warm amber ambient back-glows rather than cold shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep Hearth `#121316`.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Containers / Recessed Panels):** Charcoal Midnight `#1A1C22` framed with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Cards, Menu Platters, Modals):** Rich Basalt `#232730` with subtle amber luminescence: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.06)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.18)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Actions, Popovers, Rooftop Domes):** `#2A2E39` with elevated gold aura: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 24px 2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.
- **Luminous Glow Overlays:** For hero banners and featured platters (e.g., Zaaki Rooftop Special Shinwari Karahi), a radial background gradient is applied: `radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12) 0%, rgba(18, 19, 22, 0) 70%)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft / Architectural** corner treatment (`roundedness: 1`). 

Geometric corner radii mimic architectural arches and carved stone masonry common in historic Afghan and Baluch architecture:
- Standard interactive elements (buttons, text inputs, chips): `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- Cards, booking summary panels, and modal containers: `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Outer dialogs and signature culinary hero frames: `12px` (`0.75rem`).
- Floating badges and status pips use full circular pill treatment (`9999px`).

- **Secondary (Burnished Outline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #D97706`, text `#FDFBF7`. Hover: background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`, border `#F59E0B`.
- **Tertiary / Rooftop VIP:** Background `#991B1B`, text `#FDFBF7`, gold hairline border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips:** Surface `#1A1C22`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#9CA3AF`. When active: surface `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #F59E0B`, text `#FDFBF7`.
- **Dietary / Origin Tags (e.g., "Kabuli Special", "Charcoal Grill"):** Text `label-sm`, uppercase tracking, background `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.2)`, text `#FBBF24`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.

### Menu Item Cards
- Surface `#232730`, 1px border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.14)`. 
- High-contrast typography: Dish title in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`, `#FDFBF7`), prices prominently styled in `title-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `#F59E0B`).
- Food photography inset with a warm dark gradient vignette (`linear-gradient(to bottom, transparent 60%, #232730 100%)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#1A1C22`, border `1px solid rgba(156, 163, 175, 0.25)`, text `#FDFBF7`, placeholder `#6B7280`.
- Focus state: border `1px solid #F59E0B`, halo glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.18)`.
- Rooftop Reservation picker features brass/gold icons for party size, date, and seating time.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Base state: `#1A1C22` background, `1px solid rgba(156, 163, 175, 0.4)`.
- Checked state: `#F59E0B` background with deep charcoal `#121316` check/dot indicator and subtle gold ring halo.
